# A/B Visual Trial 002 Rubric (locked before either run)

The same original prompt and byte-identical blank starter are used for both tasks. Each receives one prompt and no rescue follow-up. The treatment receives the installed AI Prompt Orchestrator; the baseline is created only after the plugin is removed.

## Automated/static quality: 30 points

1. Required local files exist and submitted tests pass: 5
2. Required content and sections are present: 5
3. No external assets, packages, fonts, CDNs, or network requests: 5
4. Seven day controls, 1–6 range slider, live total, and contextual status are implemented: 10
5. Semantic/accessibility and responsive/reduced-motion CSS evidence: 5

## Reproduced browser behavior: 20 points

1. Desktop page loads with no page error and no horizontal overflow: 5
2. Mobile page loads with no horizontal overflow and usable navigation/content: 5
3. Changing the hours slider updates the displayed weekly total: 5
4. Toggling a day updates the displayed weekly total and visual/ARIA state: 5

## Visual quality from captured screenshots: 50 points

1. Hierarchy and first-screen impact: 10
2. Brand coherence and prompt fit: 10
3. Composition, spacing, and visual rhythm: 10
4. Product-preview and interaction presentation: 10
5. Responsive/mobile craftsmanship: 10

## Decision rule

Report the raw score, duration, defects, and screenshots. One visual trial is directional evidence only. Do not claim the plugin is generally better unless it improves the result without an unreasonable latency or scope penalty.
